02 · METHODOLOGY

How we ran the research

01
Scan the market
Three parallel scouts swept Gumroad, Notion Marketplace, G2, Crunchbase, Hacker News, GitHub, Reddit, IndieHackers. Goal: every dashboard with public proof of life.
02
Verify with numbers
No vibes. Every entry has a real signal — Gumroad sales, ARR, funding, G2 review count, GitHub stars, HN points. Gaps flagged "no public number."
03
Score 1–10 × 4 axes
Revenue/traction · Social proof · Feature completeness · Design quality. Equal 25% weights. Final score = weighted average.
04
Cliff analysis
Top 5 vs bottom 5. What patterns appear in the winners that are absent in the losers? Those patterns become your build's design DNA.

03 · SCORING RUBRIC

The judging matrix

Four dimensions, equally weighted. Every dashboard scored 1–10 against each.

Revenue / Traction
25%
Gumroad sales count · ARR · funding raised · customer count · marketplace rank. The market votes with money.
Social Proof + Virality
25%
G2 / Capterra rating × review count · HN points · Reddit upvotes · GitHub stars · creator audience size. Reach × intensity.
Feature Completeness
25%
Coverage across clients · finance · projects · team · KPIs · personal/habits · integrations. How much of the founder's life it covers.
Design + UX Quality
25%
Visual polish · information hierarchy · daily-use friction · screenshot legibility. Qualitative — the eye test you can defend.
